Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is the minimum deposit? +
The minimum deposit is $100 for the Startup plan, $1,000 for Pro, and $5,000 for Ultimate.
How long before I can withdraw? +
You can request a withdrawal at any time. However, investment profits are released when your plan matures (24h, 48h, or 72h).
Is my capital safe? +
Yes. Your initial deposit is returned in full at the end of the investment period. Profits are paid on top of your capital.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We currently accept USDT (BEP20 and TRC20). More payment options will be added soon.
